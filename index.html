ABOUT THE PROJECT

What is 360Alert?

360Alert focuses on using modern technology to make safety monitoring more intelligent, responsive and easier to manage.

The platform combines AI-driven event detection, real-time monitoring and centralized alert management to provide better visibility into safety-related events.

Its applications can extend across healthcare, elderly care, education and other environments where proactive safety monitoring is valuable.
